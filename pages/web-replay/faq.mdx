# Frequently Asked Questions

## Project Management  

**What are tenantId and appId?**  
- **tenantId** is the unique identifier for your organization, with each organization assigned exactly one tenantId.  
- **appId** is the unique identifier for an application under your organization, used to distinguish individual products (e.g., websites/client apps).  

**How do I obtain tenantId and appId?**  
- Contact the Softprobe team to request allocation of your unique tenantId and appId.  

## Technical Solutions  

**Is mobile H5 page supported?**  
- Fully compatible with mobile browsers, including complete capture and replay support for gestures (pinch-to-zoom, long-press).  

**What is the data retention period?**  
- Data is retained for 365 days by default. For custom retention policies or database specifications, contact the Softprobe technical team.
