# Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our platform.

## General

### What is this platform?

Our platform is a comprehensive SaaS solution that helps businesses manage their data, integrate with third-party services, and build powerful applications.

### How do I get started?

Check out our [Getting Started Guide](/docs/getting-started/installation) for a step-by-step walkthrough.

### Is there a free tier?

Yes! We offer a free tier with:
- 1,000 API requests per month
- 1 project
- Basic support

### What are the pricing plans?

We offer three plans:

- **Free**: $0/month - Perfect for testing
- **Pro**: $29/month - For growing businesses
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing - For large organizations

## Technical

### How do I authenticate with the API?

Use API keys in the Authorization header. See our [Authentication Guide](/docs/api/authentication) for details.

### What are the rate limits?

Rate limits vary by plan:
- Free: 1,000 requests/hour
- Pro: 10,000 requests/hour
- Enterprise: Custom limits

### What programming languages are supported?

We provide SDKs for:
- JavaScript/Node.js
- Python
- PHP
- Ruby

### How do I handle errors?

All errors follow a consistent format. Check our [API Reference](/docs/api/endpoints) for error codes and handling.

## Billing

### How does billing work?

Billing is monthly and based on your usage. You're charged at the beginning of each month.

### Can I change my plan?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your dashboard.

### Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all paid plans.

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards and PayPal.
